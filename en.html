FAQ

The important questions

What businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses in the US and Canada. My deepest specialization is cleaning

Do you work outside cleaning?

Yes. I also work with other local niches when results can be measured and the service economics are clear enough

What is included in the fee?

The exact scope is agreed before launch. The baseline scope is shown above without invented packages or prices

Is ad spend included in your fee?

Ad spend and my fee are agreed separately before launch

What do you need from me?

Access, business materials and feedback on inquiry quality. The exact list depends on the project

How do you evaluate results?

Using advertising metrics and inquiry quality. Bookings and sales are evaluated separately when the business shares that data

Who handles inquiries?

We agree this before launch. Advertising can generate inquiries, but response speed and sales handling also affect the outcome

How does exclusivity work?

Cleaning only: while we work together, I do not take a direct competitor in the agreed territory for the agreed service type

What if my territory is already occupied?

I first check whether there is a real conflict by service type and area. If there is, I do not launch a direct competitor in that territory
